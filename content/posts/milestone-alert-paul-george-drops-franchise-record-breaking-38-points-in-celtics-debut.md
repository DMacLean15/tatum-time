On October 20th, 2026 at Little Caesars Arena In Detroit, Paul George had a record breaking output dropping an eye-popping 38 Points in his Celtics debut. His final line included: 38 Points (12-28 FG), (7-12 3PM), (7-8 FT), 5 Rebounds, 3 Assists and 5 Steals. During the momentous event, he also passed Kyle Korver to grab a solo hold of 8th for most 3PM in NBA History.
