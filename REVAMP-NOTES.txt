================================================================
  TATUM TIME - AUGUST 2026 REVAMP  (what changed and why)
================================================================
Everything that makes the site Dan's is untouched: the banners, the
basketball cursor, the game-day anthem, the shot clock, the green.
This pass made it faster, cleaner, and gave every post a real page.

----------------------------------------------------------------
LOOK & FEEL (index.html)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Neon glows, the pulsing "Hear Dan's Take" button, the blinking
  GAME DAY title, and the spinning close button are gone - the
  Celtics identity now reads broadcast-graphics, not arcade.
- The sci-fi Orbitron font is replaced by a clean monospace for
  the countdown clocks; in-article headings switched from Anton
  to bold Inter (Anton stays for the hero, section titles, brand,
  banners and big stat numbers).
- Articles read like a magazine: comfortable line length (70ch),
  larger line height, refined drop cap.
- New: visible keyboard-focus outlines and a prefers-reduced-motion
  fallback (accessibility).

----------------------------------------------------------------
BOLD PASS (round 2 - homepage energy)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- NEW courtside ticker under the nav, like a broadcast strip:
  last result (W/L + score + opponent logo), next tip-off
  countdown, and live record / East seed / streak. It shows in
  the offseason too; on game day it steps aside for the green
  GAME DAY banner. Built entirely from the ESPN data the site
  already fetches - no new services.
- Parquet floor: a faint gold herringbone weave behind the hero
  and the Franchise section. Pure CSS, unmistakably the Garden.
- Tighter vertical rhythm: section padding 64px -> 44px, hero and
  headers compressed - roughly a full screen less scrolling.
- The hero's "Posts & counting" number is now live (real count).

----------------------------------------------------------------
SHORTER PAGE (round 3 - structure)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Headlines is now a TAB inside Celtics Central (was its own full
  section); it loads only when opened.
- The roster shows the top 8 players with a "Show the full roster"
  button (player-name links in posts auto-expand it). Cuts ~4
  screens off the default page.
- The newsletter band moved inside the About section (the
  id="newsletter" anchor still works).
- Spacing now at blog rhythm: sections 32px (24px on phones),
  smaller section headers. The homepage is roughly HALF its old
  scroll length.

----------------------------------------------------------------
POLISH (round 4 - small optimizations + QoL)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Reading-progress bar: a thin green line under the nav fills as
  you read the open article (only visible while reading).
- Native share button on phones (opens the real iOS/Android share
  sheet) next to the existing X/Facebook/Threads buttons.
- Back-to-top button appears after you scroll a ways.
- Post search now searches the FULL text of every article (it
  previously only matched the first 140 characters).
- Preconnect hints for ESPN + Supabase - live scores and comments
  start their connections during page load.
- NEW _headers file (commit it too): browser caching for
  js/images/mp3 + basic security headers.

----------------------------------------------------------------
ADMIN PAGE (round 5 - Dan's editor, admin/index.html)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- The six stacked floating buttons became ONE "☘️ Toolkit" button
  opening a tabbed panel: Recipes / Roster / Emojis / Links /
  Ideas, with View Live Site + Formatting Help at the top. Much
  cleaner, and it finally works on a phone.
- NEW "💡 Ideas" tab: a shuffle deck of 20 Celtics story prompts
  for blank-page days.
- NEW live word count + read time chip that appears while Dan
  writes (shows the same "X min read" the site will).
- Scoreboard (bottom-left) upgrades: a Season line (record, East
  seed, streak) and a second magic button - "Copy last-game stats
  link" - to fill the Game Box-Score Link field in one click.
- The login screen is Celtics dark green instead of plain white.
- Help sheet updated to match all of the above.
NOTE: the Toolkit only appears after login, so the full test is on
tatumtime.net/admin after deploy (or locally via npx decap-server).

----------------------------------------------------------------
SPEED
----------------------------------------------------------------
- marked.js is now self-hosted (js/marked.min.js) and loads with
  defer - it no longer blocks the first paint. The SAME file also
  powers build-pages.js at deploy time.
- EmailJS only loads at the moment someone actually comments or
  subscribes (it used to load for every visitor, render-blocking).
- The built-in fallback posts were trimmed from 10 full articles
  to 1 (they only ever show when the site is opened from disk).
- og.png (259 KB) replaced by og.jpg (32 KB) for link previews.
- Google Fonts trimmed (dropped Orbitron + one Inter weight).
- All photos inside articles now lazy-load.

----------------------------------------------------------------
EVERY POST NOW HAS ITS OWN PAGE  (big SEO/share win)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- NEW build-pages.js generates post/<slug>/index.html for every
  published post, each with its own title, description and photo
  for link previews - plus a full sitemap.xml.
- build-rss.js runs it automatically, so the existing Cloudflare
  build command needs NO changes.
- Share buttons and the RSS feed now point at these real pages;
  each page links back to the homepage reader for comments.
- Scheduled (future-dated) posts do not get a page until a deploy
  happens on/after their date.

----------------------------------------------------------------
NEW READER FEATURES (index.html)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- "More from Tatum Time": 3 related posts (same category first)
  under every article.
- Year dividers appear in the post list automatically once the
  archive spans more than one year.

----------------------------------------------------------------
OPTIONAL UPGRADES (dormant until you set them up)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- SETUP-COMMENT-MODERATION.txt - comments wait for Dan's approval.
  (Run the SQL, then flip moderated:true in commentsConfig.)
- SETUP-NEWSLETTER-EMAILS.txt - actually email subscribers when a
  post drops (functions/send-newsletter.js, needs Resend + 4 env
  vars in Cloudflare; shows "not configured" until then).
